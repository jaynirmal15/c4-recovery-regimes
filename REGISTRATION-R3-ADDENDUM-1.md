# R3 addendum 1: one row of the primary-rule table covers two quantities

Dated 2026-10-09, after the registration (commit 4b53676) and the scoring code (a87c9d8), before the
shakedown and before any trial. The registration's text is not edited; this file is read with it. It
changes no prediction, no rule and no line of the scoring code.

The primary-rule table has one row for the 25 s arm at 18, 19 and 26 s, with "26.73 to 27.25 s" under
"Last request inside the attempt leaves at". That figure is correct for what the column defines: it is
the arm's last request inside the attempt, and so the arm's first-response boundary, which the 26 and
28 s cells test. At 19 s the first response is the reply to that same request, at about 26.9 s. At
18 s the first response comes earlier, from the request at 11 units, 18.47 to 18.99 s after onset, as
the survival table already states.

So the row covers two quantities. The 26 and 28 s cells test where the first-response boundary lies.
The 18 and 19 s cells in this arm test the survival outcome, where the server's removal binds and the
schedule does not; for the primary rule all three of 18, 19 and 26 s are predicted to receive a
response and only the time and type of that response differ between them.

Also dated here, a change to the shakedown, made before it ran: a fourth trial, the 15 s arm at 18 s,
so that one shakedown record written by the new client carries an accepted response. The shakedown
stays apart from the 100 and is not scored with them.
