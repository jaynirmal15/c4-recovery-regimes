# R4 post-registration note 1: which test the run is powered for

Written 2026-10-12T02:41:43Z, **after the run had begun** (the session started at 2026-10-12T02:11:00Z and nine of the
ten LiveKit trials had been recorded when this was written; none had been scored or opened). It is not
part of the registration and does not amend it. The registration's text, predictions, band and scoring
rules are unchanged. It is recorded so that two purposes of the run are not conflated when it is
written up, and the score says that it was written after the run began.

The twenty trials serve two purposes with very different power.

1. **Primary: does a corrected instrument reproduce the behaviour.** Ten restarts on LiveKit at 17 s and
   ten ride-outs on Janus at 5 s, with detection in the band. This is answered whether or not the defect
   appears in the run, and it is what the run is powered for.
2. **Secondary: does the correction catch the defect live.** This is tested only in trials where the
   defect occurs. At the recorded rates, 3.9% of LiveKit and 2.5% of Janus full-isolation trials, the
   chance of at least one such trial is about 33% in the LiveKit arm, 22% in the Janus arm and 48% across
   both: a little under even. The registration's informativeness criterion scores it; a run with no
   informative trial leaves the secondary question unanswered and is not evidence for the correction.

A success on the primary purpose must not be written as a success on the secondary one.
